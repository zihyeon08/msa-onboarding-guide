## What is the Student Ambassadors program?

Microsoft Student Ambassadors is a global community of students who are passionate about building AI-powered solutions with Microsoft technology and accelerating innovation. Students who register for the program gain opportunities to explore cutting-edge technology, participate in AI competitions, lead local communities, and grow their online influence. By connecting with tech enthusiasts, AI innovators, and future founders around the world, they help advance Microsoft’s mission of empowering everyone and every organization to achieve more.

## MSA Direction

### 1. The core purpose of the program

* The core direction of Microsoft Student Ambassadors is to help students grow through Microsoft technology, grow together with other students, and prove that growth through real actions and results.
* This is not simply a learning program for studying technology. It aims to help students shape their future proactively and become people who create meaningful change with technology.

### 2. Why the Student Ambassadors program exists

* The official page starts from the following problem statement:
* Technology is evolving rapidly today, and students need not only knowledge but also practical skills and leadership experience.
* This kind of growth is stronger in a community than it is alone.
* Therefore, MSA is designed to turn an individual student’s potential into proof through real actions and results.

### 3. Why the program goes beyond technical learning and focuses on leadership

* MSA is not meant to create students who merely “know Microsoft technology well.”
* The official page clearly emphasizes the following direction:
  * Learning how to use AI and Microsoft technology creatively and responsibly.
  * Using technology not alone, but sharing it with others and growing together.
  * Naturally building leadership and influence through that process.
* In short, MSA’s purpose is to grow three things at the same time: technical skill, leadership, and responsible influence.

### 4. Why community-centered growth is emphasized

* The keyword repeated throughout the official introduction is Community.
* MSA explains that student connections help participants challenge one another, support one another, and grow faster and deeper than they could alone.
* Here, community is not just socializing. It means an environment where people develop ideas through collaboration, build confidence through mentorship, and grow together.

### 5. Why “proven growth” matters

* MSA does not aim for participation without results.
* Some milestones show the path you have already taken, and others show where you are going next.
* This process leaves clear evidence that can lead to a résumé and a career.
* In other words, MSA helps students prove that they are not just “prepared” people, but people who have already done something meaningful.

### 6. What it means that the program is open

* The Student Ambassadors program is open to students from all backgrounds and does not require complex screening or special qualifications.
* What matters is not the starting line, but what actions you take and what contribution you make.
* The more you participate, act, and grow, the more opportunities expand.

### 7. Official summary of the MSA direction

* Microsoft Student Ambassadors is a global student community program designed to help students learn through Microsoft technology, grow with their community, and prove their growth and contributions through real actions and milestones.
* The program aims to help students shape their own future through technical skill, leadership, collaboration, and responsible AI usage.
* It is open to all students and does not require separate screening or gatekeeping.
* What matters is not your background, but the actions you take after joining.

## What do you receive when you become an Ambassador?

Registered members can access a global Discord community of students, advisors, and mentors, along with resources, guidance, and learning to grow their skills in areas that interest them. Onboarded Student Ambassadors can also access a global Microsoft Teams community made up of Student Ambassadors, Microsoft employees, and Microsoft MVPs, and receive program certificates, milestones, and badges. They also receive Office 365, Visual Studio Enterprise, and monthly Azure credits.

## What values and standards must registered members follow?

By submitting the Student Ambassadors program registration, you are considered to have agreed to Microsoft’s privacy policy and to have confirmed your eligibility for the program. All registered students must meet the following requirements:

* Be at least 18 years old at the time of registration
* Be enrolled full-time at an accredited school (for example, a junior college or university)
* Have an Azure for Students account
* Not be a previous registrant or Ambassador removed from the program by Microsoft
* Not be a Microsoft employee or current contractor

By signing the registered member handbook site in SharePoint, you also agree to Microsoft’s services terms and registered member rules.

## How does Ambassador activity help you in school and in your career?

You can gain tangible achievements and experiences that you can include in your résumé and LinkedIn profile. You can demonstrate specific outcomes such as:

* Building technical skills by creating AI-powered solutions using Microsoft AI and cloud technologies
* Growing leadership skills by hosting events that introduce Microsoft technologies, products, and services
* Growing your online presence by sharing Microsoft content, increasing engagement, and expanding your reach

You can also expand your professional network by forming new relationships with other students, Microsoft employees, and Microsoft MVPs.
