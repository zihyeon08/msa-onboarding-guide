# Registration Guide

## Registration Process (Steps 1–5)

* **Step 1 Registration:** Sign up at the Student Ambassadors portal. You must be at least 18 years old and enrolled full-time at an accredited school.
* **Step 2 Contributor ID:** You will receive your Contributor ID by email within 3–5 business days after registration. This ID gives you access to the registered member handbook.
* **Step 3 Path Selection:** After receiving your Contributor ID, choose a path: Community Influencer | Community Builder
* **Step 4 Earn a Credential:** Before onboarding, earn a Microsoft Certification or complete at least one Microsoft Applied Skills Assessment within 12 months.
* **Step 5 Reporting and Progress Updates:**
  * Community Influencer | Every Saturday, Pacific Time
  * Community Skiller | Every Saturday, Pacific Time
  * Reporting emails are sent weekly and may take up to 48 hours to fully process. Activities completed within 48 hours of the reporting window may not appear immediately.
* **Need help?** For Discord-related or registration issues, contact `discord@studentambassadors.com`.
  > **Note:** The Discord operations team works in Pacific Time (PST) and replies within 1–2 business days. Please avoid sending duplicate emails for the same issue.

## What should you do after registering?

Students visit StudentAmbasssadors.com and click [Get Started] to register for the community. Registration is quick and simple and can be completed in under 5 minutes. After registering, you complete pre-defined activities aligned with your interests and skills to become a Student Ambassador. These activities may include building AI-powered solutions, hosting community events, and sharing social media content. Choose the registration path that best matches your interests and skills (Community Influencer or Community Skiller), and complete the pre-defined activities for that path to become a Student Ambassador. Registered members who meet the activity requirements receive an invitation to the Student Ambassadors program. Once you accept the invitation, you are onboarded and receive the official title, certificate, and Student Ambassadors member benefits, including an @studentambassadors.com email address and Microsoft Teams access.

## What is the Student Ambassadors registration path?

The registration paths (Community Influencer or Community Skiller) are designed around two broad interest and skill categories so that students can focus on what matters most in their journey to becoming a Student Ambassador. Choose a path and complete the pre-defined activities for that path to become a Student Ambassador.

| COMMUNITY INFLUENCER PATH | COMMUNITY SKILLER PATH |
|---|---|
| Community Influencer wants to grow online influence by creating and sharing Microsoft content to attract audiences and increase engagement. | Community Skiller wants to build leadership skills by hosting Student Learn Plans that include Microsoft training content. |

## How long does it take to become an Ambassador?

We know you have a busy life and many responsibilities. That is why the program supports you in completing registration activities on your own schedule. The pace at which you become a Student Ambassador is up to you. Onboarded Student Ambassadors receive guidance on when to complete their activities, but the timeline is designed with student schedules in mind. You are free to manage your calendar.

---

## How do I cancel my Student Ambassadors registration?

If you are no longer interested in becoming an Ambassador, follow the steps below to cancel your registration. Canceling your registration does not affect your Student Ambassadors social media membership.

* Go to the Student Ambassadors site and log in with your registration account
* Select the profile circle in the top-right corner, then choose My Account
* In the left menu, choose Privacy and click Delete Data in the red box
* Enter your name as shown and click Delete My Data
