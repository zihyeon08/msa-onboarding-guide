# Community Influencer Program Path

Community Influencers want to grow their online presence by creating and sharing Microsoft content to attract audiences, drive engagement, and build relationships. Registered members who choose the Community Influencer program path must complete the check-marked activities below to be invited to onboard into the program. Invitations are sent quarterly in January, April, July, and October.

- Create and share content using your Contributor ID to reach 250 Preferred Visitors on eligible Microsoft URLs
- Earn a Microsoft Certification or Microsoft Applied Skills credential within 12 months before onboarding

---

## What is Contributor ID and how do I get it?

Students who register for the Student Ambassadors program receive a Contributor ID to use when completing required program path activities. This ID is used to track progress toward becoming a Student Ambassador, and it does not change for as long as you remain a registered member. Students receive their Contributor ID by email within 3–5 days after registering for the program.

The general format is shown below, with `#` replaced by unique numbers:

- **Contributor ID format:** `?wt.mc_id=studentamb_######`

> **Important:** Anyone with a Learn profile also has a Learn Contributor ID. You can find it in the Learn profile settings under Contributor ID. Make sure you use your Student Ambassadors Contributor ID, not your Learn Contributor ID.

---

## How do I add my Contributor ID to content URLs?

Students who choose the Community Influencer path share content with online communities by appending their Contributor ID to the end of eligible Microsoft URLs.

For a step-by-step process and a final sharing checklist, see [Creating a Contributor URL](08-contributor-url.md).

- If an eligible Microsoft URL includes a language-region code (for example, `en-us/`, `fr-fr/`, and so on), remove the language-region code before appending your Contributor ID.
- If an eligible Microsoft URL already contains a `?` (which indicates another Contributor ID), replace the `?` with `&` before adding your ID to the end of the URL.

**Example:**

1. Link you want to share: `https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot?WT.mc_id=academic`
2. Remove the language-region code to update the link: `https://learn.microsoft.com/copilot?WT.mc_id=academic`
3. Replace `?` with `&` and add your Contributor ID to the end of the URL
4. New link: `https://learn.microsoft.com/copilot?WT.mc_id=academic&wt.mc_id=studentamb_######`

## What are Preferred Visitors?

Preferred Visitors is a proprietary metric, so the exact dimensions are not public. When sharing content, Community Influencers should focus on the broader purpose of the path — attracting an audience, driving engagement, and building relationships — to create a valuable experience.

| Experience              | Example                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Valuable experience** | A registered member regularly shares certification-related content on LinkedIn and posts a TechCommunity blog link about a recently published FreeCodeCamp certification course with their Contributor ID. Their first-degree connections click and read the blog. Several of them reshare the content. Second-degree connections click and read the blog and begin following the original poster. |
| **Not valuable**        | A registered member spams a link into a chat room on WhatsApp and asks people to click it. The message is not related to a specific interest or skill and is quickly pushed up the chat list.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

## Execution tips

**Task:** Share content, updates, posts, and blogs related to Microsoft technologies.

If your count is not increasing, review the guidance below first. Public visibility, relevant content, regular curation, and community engagement matter more than simply sharing more links.

1. Share only on **open social networks** such as Facebook, Instagram, Reddit, X, YouTube, LinkedIn, and blogs. Messaging apps and email do not count. The posts must be publicly visible to the SA Team, so this is required.
2. Regularly rotate through the various links provided on the websites in the handbook, not the homepages. Explore the websites and social media themselves to find technologies that are interesting to your community.
3. Avoid homepage links! Also build a regular posting habit. Preferably share curated posts weekly or every other week for the links you share.
4. Share no more than 2–3 links per post, or the content may be flagged as spam.
5. **Create curated content** for the links you share. Do not ask for counts.
6. **Do not use third-party URL shorteners such as Bitly.** They may interfere with the original URL's Contributor ID tracking and the count system.
7. Keep an active, large community/following so you gain more Preferred Visitors each week. Closed accounts and small networks can slow or stall your progress.
8. Tag other users in the post and ask them to reshare it in their networks. This helps reach and works especially well on LinkedIn.
9. Microsoft Learn Plans are not eligible for the Community Influencer path.

## How do I check my Preferred Visitors count?

Reporting emails are sent weekly and may take up to 48 hours to finish processing. Activities completed within 48 hours of the email send time may not be included in that week’s count. You can also check the count on the Progression Board, which is a me-only screen that shows only your own progress. The Progression Board is updated at the same time the reporting email is sent.

- If you have not received your first reporting email, the me-only screen will be empty.
- If you have received your first reporting email, the me-only screen will show your count.

> **Preferred Visitors update (2025-11-03):** Preferred Visitors is a proprietary metric, so its dimensions are not public. Focus on creating a valuable experience by attracting an audience, driving engagement, and building relationships.

| Program path         | Reporting email sent         | Progression Board updated    |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Community Influencer | Every Saturday, Pacific Time | Every Saturday, Pacific Time |
| Community Skiller    | Every Saturday, Pacific Time | Every Saturday, Pacific Time |

## Why is the count different from what I expected?

If the reported Preferred Visitor count is different from what you expected, the content may not have been shared as a valuable experience.

> **Important:** Spam, scripting, or any activity that violates the Microsoft Services Agreement, the registered member rules, or other terms agreed to during registration and/or SharePoint site sign-in is prohibited. Microsoft reserves the right to ask all registered members to verify their Community Influencer counts.
