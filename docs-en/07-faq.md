# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Why are counts not being reflected for the Community Influencer path?**
* **A.** Make sure your Contributor ID (`?wt.mc_id_xxxxx`) is attached to the end of the URL. Also make sure you are following the guidance in the registered member handbook and Discord posts.

**Q2. Why are counts not being reflected for the Community Skiller path?**
* **A.** Make sure your Contributor ID (`?wt.mc_id_xxxxx`) is attached to the end of the URL. Also make sure your Contributor ID is included in the plan description, and that you are following the guidance in the registered member handbook and Discord posts.

**Q3. I am just getting started. What should I do?**
* **A.** Congratulations on starting your journey with Microsoft Student Ambassadors (MSA)! To become an MSA, students must complete pre-defined activities aligned with their interests and skills according to the program rules. Check the resources sent to you by email, including the registered member handbook.

**Q4. I completed all the tasks. When will I be onboarded?**
* **A.** Congratulations on completing the first step to becoming an MSA! The SA program reviews students who meet the onboarding requirements quarterly. The deadline for each quarter is the last day of that quarter, and onboarding happens at the start of the following quarter. The program quarters are as follows:
  * **Jan–Mar** (progress through Mar 31 at 11:59 PM PST; onboarding in April)
  * **Apr–Jun** (progress through Jun 30 at 11:59 PM PST; onboarding in July)
  * **Jul–Sep** (progress through Sep 30 at 11:59 PM PST; onboarding in October)
  * **Oct–Dec** (progress through Dec 31 at 11:59 PM PST; onboarding in January)

**Q5. I could not complete the requirements within the quarter.**
* **A.** No worries! Your progress will roll over to the next quarter. To avoid account deactivation, make sure you continue making progress according to the program requirements.

**Q6. What options do I have for Microsoft credentials?**
* **A.** The Microsoft SA program accepts two credentials as one of the requirements.
  * **Microsoft Certification Exam:** A proctored, paid exam. Microsoft Learn also offers GitHub credentials, which are accepted as well. Microsoft regularly runs discount voucher promotions for certification exams, so keep an eye on the Learn website, LinkedIn, and social channels.
  * **Microsoft Applied Skills:** A free, hands-on assessment without a proctor.

**Q7. My MVP profile says “Under Review.” What does that mean?**
* **A.** All registrants remain in “Under Review” status until they complete all the tasks required to become an Ambassador and are onboarded into the program. If you see this status, there is no need to worry — just complete the tasks according to the registered member handbook.

**Q8. I cannot access my registration in the MVP portal.**
* **A.** All registrants are required to have an Azure for Students account and link it in the MVP portal.
  * **Steps:**
    1. Log in to the MVP portal (`https://mvp.microsoft.com/studentambassadors.com`).
    2. Click the profile tile in the top right and choose My Account.
    3. Complete the Azure For Students process. (Apply for Azure for Students)
    4. Your registration will become accessible after Step 3 is completed.
